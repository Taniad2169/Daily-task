How we work together

  1. We stay in touch. Once Cambria Dental decides to move forward, we stay in communication through the whole process. You can also choose one person from your team as the point of contact. That is who I will update on the progress, ask questions and request any additional information from. Please add their name, title, email and phone number in the form below.
  2. You see every update. During the build you will receive a preview link. The link always shows the latest version, so you can see each change as it happens.
  3. Final walk-through. Before launch we review the website together: functionality, brand colors, buttons, content, titles and pictures. This is the time to request any edits.
  4. Connecting your domain. Once both parties approve the website, we connect it to your domain. Your domain is managed by a third-party provider (for example, the company that hosted your previous website). You can contact them to connect the new website, or we can help you with it. Please keep in mind that the domain provider is a separate company and is not associated with The WebSwan, so any issue with the domain itself needs to be addressed with the domain provider.
  5. Refund policy. Once the website is ready, tested and fully functional, with all buttons working, Cambria Dental may request a 30% partial refund.
  6. Your website, your access. When the website is complete, I will assign it to you with your own login credentials, so you can make changes such as adding or removing pictures, changing brand colors and updating content. I do not advise changing the written content, because all content is SEO-optimized for search engines.
  7. Ongoing support (optional). If you would like The WebSwan as your digital agency, we can discuss ongoing services in the future: social media marketing, monthly Google Business Profile (GBP) management to keep you relevant in Google search, website edits, and monitoring your state attorney general for changes in website regulations.
