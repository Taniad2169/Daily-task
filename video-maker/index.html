This is NOT your video voiceover. Just read anything for 20–60 seconds so it learns your voice and your accent. Then any script you write can be spoken in your voice. Here's something easy to read:

"Hi, I'm Tania with The WebSwan. We help doctors and attorneys get found online, keep their websites compliant, and never miss a call. I love what I do, because every practice has a story, and my job is to help them tell it. When you're ready, give us a call, and let's talk about your goals for this year."
